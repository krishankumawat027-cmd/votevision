Voter Rights Guide

Know your rights as an Indian Citizen.

Right to Vote

Every citizen aged 18+ has the fundamental right to vote without discrimination.

Right to Privacy

The secrecy of your ballot is guaranteed. Nobody can force you to reveal who you voted for.

Right to Information

Voters have the right to know the financial and criminal background of candidates.

Right to Complaint

You can complain against any electoral malpractice, fraud, or violation of the Model Code of Conduct.

Right Not to Vote (NOTA)

You have the right to register a "None of the Above" vote if you feel no candidate is suitable.
